Delete Account

Cha-Ching Game

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your Cha-Ching account and what happens to your data when you do.

01What Happens When You Delete

In Short: All your personal data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

When you delete your Cha-Ching account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email, avatar)
  • Your notification preferences and history
  • Your device tokens and push notification settings
  • Your room memberships
  • Your chip balance and transaction history
  • Your bet stakes and outcome submissions

Note: To preserve room history integrity, your past bets will be anonymized (your identity removed) rather than deleted entirely. This ensures other members can still see their betting history.

02How to Delete Your Account

Delete from the App

  1. Open the Cha-Ching app on your device
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted
This action is permanent. Once you delete your account, all your data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Make sure you want to proceed before confirming deletion.
